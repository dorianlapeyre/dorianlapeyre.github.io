Surf lifeguard

SMGBL — Capbreton beaches (Landes, France) — 6 seasons, 2021 – 2026

Progression and duties

2025 – 2026
Grade 5-6		La Centrale Beach, Capbreton
  • Management of a lifeguard station.
  • Decision-making in emergency situations as the leader of a team of 11 Surf Lifeguards.
  • Enforcement of beach regulations on a high-crowd beach in Capbreton.
2024 – 2025
Grade 3		La Piste Beach, Capbreton
  • Team management and organization of the swimming area.
  • Decision-making in emergency situations and coordination with the station captain/co-captains.
  • Maintenance of rescue equipment and radio communications.
  • Autonomy during major interventions.
  • Management of emergency situations with a small crew.
2023 – 2024
Grade 2		La Piste Beach, Capbreton
  • First aid procedures and victim care.
  • Active rescues in the swimming zone (rescue tube, rescue board).
  • Public prevention regarding ocean dangers.
  • Autonomy during small and medium interventions.
  • Inventory/management of rescue and first aid equipment.
2021 – 2022
Grade 1		La Piste Beach, Capbreton
  • First aid procedures and victim care.
  • Active rescues in the swimming zone (rescue tube, rescue board, jet ski).
  • Public prevention regarding ocean dangers.
